Beginner Tips for Staying Safer Online

Published August 2026 · Daily Tips Hub

Good online safety begins with ordinary habits.

1. Start with the basics

Use unique, strong passwords for important accounts and enable multi-factor authentication where available.

2. Make it practical

Be cautious with unexpected links, attachments and messages asking for passwords, codes or urgent payments. When unsure, open the service through its official app or website instead of following the message link.

3. Keep it simple

Keep your phone, browser and important apps updated so you receive current security fixes.
