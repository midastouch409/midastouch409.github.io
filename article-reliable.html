How to Find Reliable Information Online

Published August 2026 · Daily Tips Hub

Not every confident-looking webpage is reliable.

1. Start with the basics

Check who published the information, when it was updated and whether the author provides evidence. For important claims, compare multiple independent sources.

2. Make it practical

Be especially careful with headlines that create urgency or promise an extreme result. A calm second look can reveal missing context.
