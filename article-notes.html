How to Take Better Notes

Published August 2026 · Daily Tips Hub

Good notes are easier to review when they capture ideas rather than every sentence.

1. Start with the basics

Start with headings and short points. Mark definitions, examples and questions separately so your future self can find them quickly.

2. Make it practical

After a learning session, write a short summary from memory. Then compare it with your notes and fill in the gaps.
